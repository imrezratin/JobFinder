Job boards without an API

BDJobs, LinkedIn, Indeed and friends don't offer a public API, so these links open a search already filled with your target title. Found something good? Use to see how well it matches.

Score any job posting

Paste a job description from BDJobs, LinkedIn, an email — anything — and get the same explainable match % plus the skills you're missing.

How the match % works. Skills found in the posting are compared with skills found in your CV (weighted; title mentions count double). Role fit checks whether the job title belongs to one of your role families (adjacent families get partial credit). Seniority compares the posting's level with your years. Location rewards Bangladesh and worldwide/Asia-friendly remote roles. Industry and title similarity add small bonuses. Hover the "Why ?%" panel on any job for the breakdown.

Job data courtesy of Remotive, Jobicy, Himalayas, RemoteOK, Arbeitnow, The Muse and, when configured, JSearch / Jooble. Listings belong to their original publishers — always apply via the original link.
